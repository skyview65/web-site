# Meeting Prep
#claudeai

## When to Use
You have a meeting in 10 minutes and need to not look unprepared.

## What I Need From You

1. **Who** am I meeting with? (name, company, role)
2. **What's the context?** (intro call, follow-up, investor, customer, etc.)
3. **What do you want** from this meeting?

## Process

### 1. Quick Research (if time)
I'll search for:
- Their LinkedIn/background
- Recent company news
- Mutual connections
- Previous interactions (if any in your notes)

### 2. Generate Prep Brief

```
## Meeting Brief: [Name] @ [Company]

**Who they are:**
[1-2 sentences on role, background, relevant context]

**Why this meeting:**
[The context/purpose]

**Your goal:**
[What you want to walk away with]

**3 things to mention:**
1. [Relevant talking point / connection point]
2. [Something about their company/work]
3. [Your hook / why they should care]

**Questions to ask:**
1. [Smart question about their situation]
2. [Question that advances your goal]
3. [Open-ended relationship builder]

**Watch out for:**
[Any potential landmines or sensitivities]
```

### 3. One-Liner Opener
"Hey [Name], thanks for making time. I saw [recent thing about them] - [brief comment]. I'm excited to [reason for meeting]."

## No Time? Minimum Viable Prep
- Know their name and company
- Know why you're meeting
- Have ONE clear ask or goal
- Have ONE good question ready
