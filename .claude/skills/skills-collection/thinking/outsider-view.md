# Outsider View
#claudeai

## The Framework

When stuck, ask: **"What would someone seeing this for the first time think?"**

Insiders have context, history, and assumptions. Sometimes these help. Sometimes they blind us.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Describe the situation as if to a stranger
Strip out jargon, history, and assumptions.
Just the facts. Just what's observable.

### Step 2: Ask outsider questions
- "What's weird about this?"
- "Why do you do it that way?"
- "Is that necessary?"
- "What would you do if starting from scratch?"

### Step 3: Notice the defensiveness
When you want to say "but you don't understand" — that's often where the insight is.

## Outsider Personas

Different outsiders see different things:

| Outsider | Asks |
|----------|------|
| **New employee** | "Why do we do it this way?" |
| **Customer** | "Why should I care?" |
| **Competitor** | "Where are they vulnerable?" |
| **Investor** | "What's the real business here?" |
| **Child** | "But why?" (repeatedly) |
| **Expert from different field** | "In [my field], we solve this by..." |

## Output Format

```
## Outsider View: [Situation]

**The insider view:**
[How you/your team sees it, with all context]

**Describing to a stranger:**
[Just the observable facts, no jargon]

**What an outsider might notice:**
- [Observation]
- [Question they'd ask]
- [Weird thing they'd point out]

**The defensive response:**
"But you don't understand, [reason]"

**Is that reason actually valid?**
[Examining the assumption]

**Outsider's likely advice:**
[What someone fresh would suggest]

**What to do with this:**
[Action or perspective shift]
```

## The Base Rate Version

Outsider view also means: "What usually happens in situations like this?"

Your situation feels unique. But statistically, similar situations have patterns.

- Most startups fail
- Most pivots don't work
- Most hires who struggle early don't improve
- Most features don't move metrics

You might be the exception. But the base rate is information.

## When to Use

- When you've been working on something too long
- When everyone agrees (might be groupthink)
- When you're defensive about criticism
- When you're about to make a big bet
- When stuck in analysis paralysis

## The Challenge

**Insiders:** "It's more complex than you understand"
**Outsiders:** "Maybe it shouldn't be"

Sometimes insiders are right. But when you've lost the ability to see it fresh, you need outside eyes.
