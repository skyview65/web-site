# Meeting Debrief
#claudeai

## When to Use
Just finished a call. Need to capture what matters before you forget.

## What I Need
Paste the transcript, your notes, or just tell me what happened.

## Process

### 1. Extract the Essentials

```
## Meeting Debrief: [Who] - [Date]

**Key Decisions Made:**
- [Decision 1]
- [Decision 2]

**Action Items:**
| Task | Owner | Due |
|------|-------|-----|
| [Task] | [You/Them] | [Date] |
| [Task] | [You/Them] | [Date] |

**Follow-up Required:**
- [ ] Send [what] to [who] by [when]
- [ ] Schedule [next meeting/check-in]

**Key Insights:**
- [Something you learned]
- [Something that surprised you]

**Open Questions:**
- [Unresolved item to address next time]
```

### 2. Draft Follow-Up Email (if needed)

```
Subject: Following up from our call

Hi [Name],

Great talking today. Quick recap:

- We agreed to [key decision]
- You're going to [their action item]
- I'll send over [your action item] by [date]

[Next step / CTA]

Talk soon,
[You]
```

## Speed Version
If you just need the essentials in 30 seconds:

**What was decided?**
**What am I doing next?**
**What are they doing next?**
**When do we talk again?**
