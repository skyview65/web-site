# Dichotomy of Control
#claudeai

## The Framework

**Some things are within our control, and some things are not.**

Within your control: Your judgments, intentions, desires, actions.
Not within your control: Everything else—outcomes, other people, external events.

Focus your energy exclusively on what you control. Accept everything else.

## The Stoic Insight

**Epictetus:** "Make the best use of what is in your power, and take the rest as it happens."

Most anxiety comes from trying to control the uncontrollable:
- Whether the investor says yes
- Whether the customer churns
- Whether the market shifts
- What people think of you
- Whether you get sick

You can influence these. You cannot control them.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Identify what's bothering you
"I'm anxious/upset about [X]"

### Step 2: Sort into categories

| In My Control | Not In My Control |
|---------------|-------------------|
| My preparation | Their decision |
| My effort | The outcome |
| My response | Their reaction |
| My integrity | Their opinion |
| What I do next | What already happened |

### Step 3: Focus only on column one
Pour your energy into what you control.
Release attachment to what you don't.

### Step 4: Reframe the goal
Instead of: "I need to close this deal"
Try: "I will prepare thoroughly and present clearly"

The first depends on them. The second depends on you.

## The Serenity Prayer (Same Idea)

"Grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, courage to change the things I can, and wisdom to know the difference."

## Common Founder Applications

| Trying to Control | Actually Control |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Whether investor funds you | Quality of your pitch |
| Whether customer buys | Quality of your product and sales process |
| What competitors do | Your response to competition |
| Market conditions | Your positioning and adaptation |
| Team member's choices | Your leadership and communication |
| Past mistakes | What you do now |

## The Practice

This isn't about being passive. It's about being effective.

Energy spent worrying about outcomes is energy not spent on actions.

Every time you catch yourself anxious about something outside your control, ask: "What CAN I do right now?"

Then do that.
