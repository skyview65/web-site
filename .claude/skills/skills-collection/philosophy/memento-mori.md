# Memento Mori (Remember Death)
#claudeai

## The Framework

**Remember that you will die.**

Not to be morbid—but to live with urgency, clarity, and proper priorities.

## The Stoic Insight

**Marcus Aurelius:** "You could leave life right now. Let that determine what you do and say and think."

**Seneca:** "Let us prepare our minds as if we'd come to the very end of life. Let us postpone nothing."

Death is the ultimate deadline. It clarifies what matters.

## How to Use

### When you're procrastinating on what matters
Ask: "If I had one year left, would I spend it on this?"

### When you're caught in trivial conflict
Ask: "Will this matter when I'm dying?"

### When you're playing it safe
Ask: "Will I regret not trying this?"

### When you're taking things for granted
Remember: "This could be the last time."

## The Questions

```
## Memento Mori Reflection

If I died in one year:
- What would I regret not doing?
- What would I stop wasting time on?
- Who would I spend more time with?
- What would I say that I haven't said?
- What would I finally start?

If this were my last:
- Last conversation with this person—what would I say?
- Last day of work—what would I prioritize?
- Last year—how would I spend it?
```

## Not Morbid—Liberating

Death meditation isn't about fear. It's about freedom.

**It frees you from:**
- Petty concerns and grudges
- Fear of others' opinions
- Postponing what matters
- Accumulating instead of experiencing
- Playing small

**It focuses you on:**
- What you actually value
- Relationships that matter
- Work that means something
- Living fully now

## Founder Applications

| Situation | Memento Mori Clarity |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Working 80 hours on low-impact tasks | Is this how I want to spend my finite time? |
| Avoiding a hard conversation | I don't have forever to resolve this |
| Playing it safe | On my deathbed, will I regret not trying? |
| Stressed about minor setback | Will this matter in 10 years? At my funeral? |
| Neglecting health/relationships | Success means nothing if I'm dead or alone |

## The Practice

Some people:
- Keep a memento mori coin or reminder
- Meditate on death briefly each morning
- Write their obituary or eulogy
- Visit cemeteries for perspective
- Set a "death clock" app

The method matters less than the regular reminder: **this ends.**

## The Paradox

**By remembering death, you come alive.**

Most people live as if they have infinite time—and waste it.

Those who remember they'll die treat time as precious—and live fully.
