# Ikigai (Reason for Being)
#claudeai

## The Framework

**Ikigai = Your reason for getting up in the morning.**

The Western adaptation frames it as the intersection of:
- What you **love**
- What you're **good at**
- What the world **needs**
- What you can be **paid for**

## The Four Circles

```
            What you LOVE
                 ○
            ╱         ╲
     What you're      What the world
     GOOD AT ○ ─────── ○ NEEDS
            ╲    ★    ╱
                 ○
         What you can
         be PAID FOR
            
        ★ = Ikigai (center)
```

## The Intersections

| Intersection | What It Feels Like |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Love + Good at | **Passion** (but might not pay or matter) |
| Good at + Paid for | **Profession** (but might feel empty) |
| Paid for + Needed | **Vocation** (but might not love it) |
| Needed + Love | **Mission** (but might not pay or be good at it) |
| All four | **Ikigai** (sustainable purpose) |

## Founder Application

Startups often start in one circle:
- **Tech founder:** Good at it + Paid for it (but may not love it or see the mission)
- **Mission-driven founder:** Love + Need (but may struggle with revenue or competence)
- **Opportunistic founder:** Paid for it (but may burn out without love or mission)

Sustainable founders eventually find all four.

## The Warning Signs

| Missing | What Happens |
|---------|--------------|
| Missing LOVE | Burnout, resentment |
| Missing SKILL | Frustration, failure |
| Missing NEED | Vanity project, no traction |
| Missing PAY | Poverty, unsustainability |

## It's a Journey, Not a Destination

Ikigai isn't something you find once. It evolves.

Your 25-year-old ikigai ≠ your 45-year-old ikigai.

What you love changes.
What you're good at grows.
What the world needs shifts.
What pays evolves.

Revisit periodically. Keep adjusting.

## The Japanese Original

Note: The four-circle diagram is a Western adaptation. Traditional ikigai is simpler—it's about small daily joys and reasons for living, not grand life purpose.

Both interpretations are useful:
- **Western:** Career/purpose framework
- **Traditional:** Daily meaning, small pleasures

You need both.
