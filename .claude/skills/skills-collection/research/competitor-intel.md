# Competitor Intelligence
#claudeai

## When to Use
You need intelligence on what competitors are doing - their strategy, positioning, pricing, or recent moves.

## What I Need
- Competitor name(s)
- What specifically do you want to know?
- What decision will this inform?

## Research Checklist

### 1. Positioning & Messaging
- [ ] Homepage headline and value prop
- [ ] How they describe themselves
- [ ] Who they say they're for
- [ ] Key differentiators they claim

### 2. Product & Features
- [ ] Core features list
- [ ] Recent launches (check blog, changelog, Product Hunt)
- [ ] What's missing vs you
- [ ] Integrations offered

### 3. Pricing & Packaging
- [ ] Pricing page (screenshot it - they change)
- [ ] Pricing model (per seat, usage, flat)
- [ ] Tier structure
- [ ] Free tier or trial details
- [ ] Enterprise/custom pricing signals

### 4. Traction & Scale
- [ ] Team size (LinkedIn)
- [ ] Funding history (Crunchbase)
- [ ] Customer logos
- [ ] Case studies and social proof
- [ ] Review volume on G2/Capterra

### 5. Go-to-Market
- [ ] Content strategy (blog, social, podcast)
- [ ] Paid ads (Facebook Ad Library, Google)
- [ ] SEO focus (what are they ranking for?)
- [ ] Outbound signals (hiring SDRs?)

### 6. Hiring = Strategy
Job postings reveal priorities:
- Lots of engineers → building
- Lots of sales → scaling GTM
- Specific roles → new products/features

## Where to Look

| Source | What You'll Find |
|--------|------------------|
| Website | Positioning, pricing, features |
| LinkedIn (company) | Headcount, growth, key hires |
| Crunchbase | Funding, investors, timeline |
| G2/Capterra | Reviews, customer sentiment |
| Product Hunt | Launch history, reception |
| Their blog | Strategy signals, content focus |
| Job boards | Hiring = priorities |
| Wayback Machine | Positioning evolution |
| Twitter/LinkedIn | Founder content, announcements |
| Facebook Ad Library | Ad creative and messaging |
