# Evaluate:

## 1. Subscription visibility
Is subscription clearly visible at product decision point?

Hidden subscription reduces adoption.

---

## 2. Subscription incentive clarity
User must clearly understand benefit:

- cost savings
- convenience
- flexibility

---

## 3. Trust and safety perception

Subscription must feel:

- safe
- reversible
- controllable

Users must believe they can easily cancel or modify.

---

## 4. Friction comparison

Subscription should be as easy or easier than one-time purchase.

---

## 5. Billing transparency

User must clearly understand:

- when billing occurs
- how often
- how much

---

# Output:

Subscription adoption friction score: 1–10

Trust score: 1–10

Conversion optimization opportunities
