name: trust-and-transparency-review
description: Evaluates whether system creates or destroys user trust.
---

# Evaluate:

- predictability
- transparency
- reversibility
- safety mechanisms

---

# Trust levels:

HIGH — safe and predictable  
MEDIUM — mostly reliable  
LOW — unsafe or confusing  

---

# Flag CRITICAL if:

- irreversible actions lack confirmation
- system acts silently
