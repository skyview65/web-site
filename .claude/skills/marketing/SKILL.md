# Marketing

Create marketing content and positioning that is specific, benefit-led, and conversion-focused.

## When to use
- Copywriting: headlines, hero/landing copy, product descriptions, CTAs.
- Channels: social posts, email, ad copy, SEO articles, newsletters.
- Strategy: positioning, value proposition, audience/persona, naming, launch angle.

## Process

1. **Anchor on audience + offer** — who it's for, what problem it solves, and the single most important benefit. If unknown, ask 1–2 sharp questions or state assumptions.
2. **Find the angle** — the sharpest, most differentiated promise. Avoid generic claims ("best-in-class").
3. **Lead with benefit, support with proof** — outcomes first; back them with features, specifics, numbers, or social proof.
4. **One clear CTA** — every piece drives a single next action.
5. **Match channel & voice** — length, tone, and format fit the medium (a tweet ≠ a landing page ≠ an SEO post).

## Frameworks (pick what fits)
- **AIDA** — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action.
- **PAS** — Problem, Agitate, Solution.
- **Value prop** — "For [audience] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], it [differentiator]."
- **Landing page** — hero (promise + CTA) → proof → how it works → objections → final CTA.

## Quality bar
- Specific over vague; show, don't assert.
- Active voice, concrete nouns, no filler or hype words.
- Accessible reading level; scannable structure (headline, subhead, bullets).
- Honest — no claims the product can't back up.
- Pairs with the `ui-ux-pro-max` skill when the copy lands in real UI.
